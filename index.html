NMIT CIE Guidelines & Evaluation Scheme

Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) split-up:

1. Non-Integrated Courses (Theory Only)

Total CIE is out of 50 marks calculated as:

  • LA-1: Learning Assignment 1 (Max 10 Marks)
  • LA-2: Learning Assignment 2 (Max 10 Marks)
  • Mid Sem-1 (MSE1): Scaled to 15 Marks (Conducted for 50)
  • Mid Sem-2 (MSE2): Scaled to 15 Marks (Conducted for 50)
CIE = LA1 (10) + LA2 (10) + MSE1 (scaled to 15) + MSE2 (scaled to 15)

2. Integrated Courses (Theory + Lab)

Total CIE is out of 50 marks calculated as:

  • Mid Sem-1 (MSE1): Scaled to 15 Marks (Conducted for 50)
  • Mid Sem-2 (MSE2): Scaled to 15 Marks (Conducted for 50)
  • Lab Marks: Continuous assessment of lab experiments + record + test (Max 20 Marks)
CIE = MSE1 (scaled to 15) + MSE2 (scaled to 15) + Lab (20)
⚠️ SEE Exam Eligibility Criterion

A student must secure a minimum of 40% of CIE marks (i.e., 20 out of 50) in each course to become eligible to appear for the Semester End Examination (SEE) in that course.
